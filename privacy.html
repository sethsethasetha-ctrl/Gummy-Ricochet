Privacy Policy

Gummy Ricochet
Effective date: October 19, 2026
App version: 1.0

About this policy

This policy describes how Gummy Ricochet handles information when you play the game. The game works offline and does not require an account.

Developer

Developer account: Jinxin Wu.

Information stored on your device

The game saves music, sound, haptic and aim-guide preferences; best scores and local leaderboard results; level progress and stars; and saved runs so you can resume playing. Run records include a random run identifier, score, duration, date, game seed and wave. Saved runs and local diagnostic records may also contain the board, skill energy, game counters and recent gameplay actions.

This information is used on your device to provide gameplay, restore runs, remember preferences and display progress and local scores. The game does not transmit these records to the developer or to third parties. Local run identifiers are used to distinguish game results, not to identify you across apps or websites.

Personal information and tracking

The game does not ask for your name, email address or other contact details. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. It does not use advertising identifiers, track you across apps or websites, or include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. The game has no in-app purchases. The developer does not collect, sell or share personal information through the game.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays in the app's storage until it is replaced during play or removed. The local leaderboard keeps up to 50 results; run identifiers used to prevent duplicate results are bounded separately. Settings and level progress persist between sessions.

To remove the game's local data, choose Delete App in iOS Settings. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. Reinstalling without restoring a backup starts with fresh local data. Device backups may retain copies of app data, and restoring a backup may restore it; manage those backups through your device or Apple account settings. The developer has no remote copy of your game records to retrieve or delete.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store activity, device backups and optional system diagnostics under its own policies and your device settings. Apple may make crash and diagnostic reports available to the developer if you enable sharing. These services are separate from the game's local storage; the game itself does not upload gameplay records. For information about Apple's processing, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The game does not request personal information from children or other players and does not include advertising or tracking. The local-data practices described in this policy apply to all players.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated with the corresponding app release. The effective date above identifies this version of the policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Jinxin Wu through the support options provided on the Gummy Ricochet App Store product page.
