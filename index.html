A colorful shooting puzzle

One bounce. A whole cascade.

Find the angle, connect matching colors, and send unsupported gummies tumbling. Take on 30 levels or build your next Endless high score.

  1. Choose one of three launchers
  2. Aim directly or bounce off a wall
  3. Match three and drop whole clusters
  4. Charge skills and plan your next shot

A little aim. Plenty of strategy.

The board keeps moving. Make space with a clever bank shot, save the right color in HOLD, and choose when to spend each skill's energy.

  • 30 levels, six chapters Learn the basics, practice four color-powered skills, and take on special challenges. Earn stars and unlock each new level through play.
  • Build an Endless run Recycle falling gummies into ammo, spend earned Credits on supplies, and choose from 24 Buffs with upgrades and powerful combinations.
  • Catch a colorful bonus Activate a reward gummy for a 30-second basket round. Collect colors for extra ammo and catch every offered gummy for a perfect-round score bonus.

Make your next bounce count.

Play offline with no account, ads or in-app purchases. Saved runs, level progress and your local leaderboard stay on your device. Music, sound, haptics and the aim guide have separate settings.
